# Mkulima AI zero-budget growth engine

## Positioning
Mkulima is the Kenyan farmer's decision companion on WhatsApp: ask naturally, get a practical next step. Do not market it as a generic chatbot or promise perfect diagnoses/prices.

## Acquisition loop
1. Collect de-identified question themes and unresolved farmer problems.
2. Rank themes by frequency, urgency and measurable usefulness.
3. Turn the top themes into short Swahili/English/local-language social scripts and farmer-group demos.
4. CTA sends the farmer to WhatsApp with a real question, not a signup form.
5. Capture source/campaign on first contact.
6. Measure question -> useful answer -> feedback -> repeat use -> referral.
7. Feed successful outcomes back into content topics and trusted knowledge review.

## Zero-budget channels
- Farmer WhatsApp groups and SACCO/co-op groups, with admin permission.
- Facebook groups/pages serving county/value-chain communities.
- TikTok/Reels/YouTube Shorts using real question formats: “Buyer amenipea 3,200, niuze?”
- Agrovets and local extension/agri-preneurs: QR/WhatsApp link at the counter.
- Farmer referrals: share Mkulima after a useful outcome.
- County/value-chain demos before broad national campaigns.

## Content pillars
Market/buyer decisions; crop symptoms; weather-to-action; inputs; post-harvest/storage; livestock; profit/cost. Content must reflect supported capabilities and label live/stale data accurately.

## Metrics
North star: farmers reaching a useful outcome, not views.
Track source, first-question completion, helpful/wrong/still-problem feedback, 7-day return, referral/share, resolved outcome, and cost (target KES 0 during validation).

## Launch sequence
Pilot one or two value chains/counties first; recruit 25 real farmers; review every failed answer; reach 70%+ explicit Helpful on reviewed pilot conversations before scaling outreach. This is an internal product target, not a claim of current performance.

## Guardrails
No invented live prices/weather/buyers. No automatic publication of private farmer conversations. De-identify examples. Health/safety-sensitive crop and livestock recommendations require trusted evidence and escalation where appropriate.
