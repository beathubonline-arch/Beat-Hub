Meet Brenda

Brenda Chepngetich is a mother, entrepreneur and financial-services professional with more than a decade of experience. She has held leadership roles and lives in Brooke, Kapsoas Ward, Ainamoi Constituency.

Her stated interests include education, girls’ empowerment, youth and women’s development, entrepreneurship, climate-smart agriculture and community service.
