About Brenda

Rooted in community. Focused on opportunity.

Brenda is a mother, entrepreneur and financial-services professional with over a decade of experience. She describes involvement in leadership roles and school visits where she mentors students, provides career guidance, discusses the challenges of adolescence and distributes sanitary pads for girls.

Read Brenda's professional and community background

Brenda lives in Brooke, Kapsoas Ward, Ainamoi Constituency. She has highlighted education, girls’ empowerment, youth and women’s development, entrepreneurship, climate-smart agriculture, community development and agricultural value addition as important areas of work.

Infrastructure

Initiatives we will advocate for

Practical priorities for water access, safer schools, health facilities and accessible trading spaces, pursued with the relevant authorities and service providers.

💧

Water connections

Advocate for water connections, pipeline extensions and boreholes in partnership with the county and relevant water service providers.

🎓

Better schools

Prioritize safe sanitation, classroom improvements, digital facilities and school lunch programmes.

✚

Better health centres

Advocate for facilities equipped with maternal and child wings and affordable-medicine wards.

▥

Modern markets

Advocate for covered market stalls, clean toilets and effective drainage systems across the wards.

Featured proposal

Kapsoas banana-processing initiative

Brenda says she has pursued a proposal for a banana-processing factory in Kapsoas through a county climate-smart agriculture programme, with the goal of agricultural value addition and local economic opportunity.
