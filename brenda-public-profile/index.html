Meet Brenda

Professional experience. Community roots.

Brenda describes herself as a mother, an entrepreneur and a professional with more than a decade of experience in financial services. She has held leadership roles and identifies Brooke in Kapsoas Ward, Ainamoi Constituency, as her home.

Her stated interests include education, girl-child empowerment, youth and women's development, entrepreneurship, community development, and practical local economic opportunity.

Her areas of focus

Education and mentorship · Girls' dignity · Youth and women's development · Entrepreneurship · Climate-smart agriculture · Agricultural value addition.
