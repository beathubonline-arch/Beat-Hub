The opportunity

Why this matters

Uasin Gishu's county planning framework includes water supply, piped connections, storage, catchment protection and climate-resilient natural-resource management. Projects need technical water-resource assessment and public-health safeguards.

These are illustrative project concepts for public discussion. They have not all been announced, budgeted, approved or delivered by Bitok.

Development portfolio

Project ideas and interventions

PROJECT 01

Piped-water expansion

Identify underserved settlements and prioritise technically feasible supply extensions.

Potential partners: Water companies, county, national agencies
PROJECT 02

Borehole and storage feasibility

Assess groundwater sustainability, water quality and water-storage needs before drilling.

Potential partners: Water specialists, regulators
PROJECT 03

Catchment and wetland protection

Map vulnerable riparian and catchment areas for restoration.

Potential partners: Environment teams, communities
PROJECT 04

Water quality monitoring

Strengthen routine testing and transparent reporting to users.

Potential partners: Water service providers, public health
PROJECT 05

Climate-smart land use

Explore erosion control, tree cover and community resilience approaches.

Potential partners: Farmers, environment partners
