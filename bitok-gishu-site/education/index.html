The opportunity

Why this matters

Education and youth skills are development priorities in Uasin Gishu. County responsibilities particularly include ECDE and village polytechnics, while many primary and secondary-school matters involve national agencies. The options below respect those distinctions.

These are illustrative project concepts for public discussion. They have not all been announced, budgeted, approved or delivered by Bitok.

Development portfolio

Project ideas and interventions

PROJECT 01

ECDE facilities

Assess safe classrooms, sanitation, play areas and learning materials for early-years learners.

Potential partners: County ECDE teams, parents
PROJECT 02

TVET and vocational pathways

Align short practical training with local demand in agro-processing, construction, repair and digital services.

Potential partners: TVET centres, employers
PROJECT 03

Career guidance

Create regular career and scholarship-information sessions for secondary and tertiary learners.

Potential partners: Schools, professionals, training institutions
PROJECT 04

Digital access and literacy

Scope public digital-learning support and trainer development.

Potential partners: ICT partners, institutions
PROJECT 05

School-to-work transitions

Offer mentorship and apprenticeship coordination where willing employers participate.

Potential partners: Employers, youth groups
Turning ideas into action

Suggested implementation roadmap

  1. 1Map training gaps and school needs
  2. 2Consult local trainers and employers
  3. 3Prepare a realistic funding and delivery framework
  4. 4Pilot courses with safeguarding controls
  5. 5Measure completion and job-relevance
