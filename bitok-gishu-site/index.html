About Julius Bitok

Public service and leadership experience

Amb. Prof. Julius Kibet Bitok has served as a Kenyan principal secretary and previously as Kenya's High Commissioner to Pakistan. His public biography records experience in finance, public administration and education. Reports published in 2026 describe his stated intention to contest the Uasin Gishu governorship in 2027.

For accuracy, this site distinguishes documented background and reported policy ideas from unconfirmed future commitments.
