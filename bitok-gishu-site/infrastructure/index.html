The opportunity

Why this matters

Better transport links, road maintenance, drainage and market access feature in county planning. Proposed interventions need ward-by-ward prioritisation, published cost estimates and coordination with the agencies legally responsible.

These are illustrative project concepts for public discussion. They have not all been announced, budgeted, approved or delivered by Bitok.

Development portfolio

Project ideas and interventions

PROJECT 01

Rural access roads

Survey all-weather access challenges for farms, schools and healthcare facilities.

Potential partners: County roads, relevant road agencies
PROJECT 02

Drainage and flood resilience

Identify recurrent flooding points in town and trading centres.

Potential partners: Engineers, environment teams
PROJECT 03

Market connections

Prioritise access to produce aggregation sites and trading centres.

Potential partners: Farmers, markets teams
PROJECT 04

Public lighting and walking safety

Evaluate pedestrian crossings, local street lighting and community safety design.

Potential partners: County and electricity agencies
PROJECT 05

Maintenance transparency

Publish maintenance schedules and simple status dashboards to help residents follow delivery.

Potential partners: County roads department, residents
Turning ideas into action

Suggested implementation roadmap

  1. 1Collect geolocated problem reports
  2. 2Rank by safety and service impact
  3. 3Review mandates, design and budget constraints
  4. 4Procure lawfully and monitor works
  5. 5Publish completion and maintenance updates
