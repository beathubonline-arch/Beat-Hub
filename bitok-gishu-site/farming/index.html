The opportunity

Why this matters

Uasin Gishu's agricultural economy depends on profitable production, reliable storage, animal health, market access and processing. Bitok's six-point agenda reported in 2022 included farm-input subsidies, cereal and cold stores and factories for maize, wheat and milk.

These are illustrative project concepts for public discussion. They have not all been announced, budgeted, approved or delivered by Bitok.

Development portfolio

Project ideas and interventions

PROJECT 01

Affordable farm inputs

Explore a targeted, transparent farm-input support programme, tied to verified farmers and published eligibility rules.

Potential partners: County agriculture office, cooperatives, national input programmes
PROJECT 02

Grain and cold storage

Map cereal depots, milk chilling and cold-chain gaps, assess upgrades and improve post-harvest storage.

Potential partners: Cooperatives, agricultural investors, regulators
PROJECT 03

Maize, wheat and dairy processing

Scope feasibility studies for milling, dairy processing and packaging, anchored in farmer supply and real market demand.

Potential partners: Manufacturers, cooperatives, enterprise agencies
PROJECT 04

Animal health and extension

Increase practical farmer training, veterinary outreach and pest-management coordination.

Potential partners: Veterinary services, extension teams
PROJECT 05

Market access and prices

Develop aggregation, commodity quality standards and buyer linkages for farmer groups.

Potential partners: Cooperatives, traders and processors
Turning ideas into action

Suggested implementation roadmap

  1. 1Publish a ward-level farmer needs and infrastructure inventory
  2. 2Conduct feasibility and environmental checks
  3. 3Cost and prioritise interventions through lawful county planning
  4. 4Run transparent pilots with measurable baselines
  5. 5Report farmer adoption, post-harvest losses and market prices
