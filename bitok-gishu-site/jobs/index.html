The opportunity

Why this matters

The county's planning documents identify enterprise support, industrial development, trade infrastructure and youth employment as areas of public need. The initiatives below are concept options for discussion, not funded programmes attributed to Bitok.

These are illustrative project concepts for public discussion. They have not all been announced, budgeted, approved or delivered by Bitok.

Development portfolio

Project ideas and interventions

PROJECT 01

Small-business support desks

Design practical permit, tax-literacy and business-registration help sessions.

Potential partners: Trade department, business associations
PROJECT 02

Enterprise finance access

Explore responsible access to lawful county funds, banks and cooperatives with clear eligibility criteria.

Potential partners: Financial institutions, cooperatives
PROJECT 03

Local supplier development

Create training pathways for eligible SMEs to improve quality, record-keeping and market access.

Potential partners: Procurement agencies, businesses
PROJECT 04

Work placements and apprenticeships

Coordinate placements and practical workplace learning with local employers.

Potential partners: TVETs, employers, youth organisations
PROJECT 05

Agro-processing jobs

Link agriculture-value-addition feasibility to employment and supply-chain skills.

Potential partners: Processors, farmer groups, training centres
Turning ideas into action

Suggested implementation roadmap

  1. 1Map wards and enterprise clusters
  2. 2Run needs assessments with entrepreneurs and employers
  3. 3Select low-cost support pilots with published criteria
  4. 4Assess financing and procurement compliance
  5. 5Track placement and business survival outcomes
