The opportunity

Why this matters

Bitok discussed an Eldoret transformation master plan in 2026 under the reported 'Agenda ni Sita na City' slogan. Public planning needs to translate city status into safer transport, attractive public spaces, reliable services and business opportunity.

These are illustrative project concepts for public discussion. They have not all been announced, budgeted, approved or delivered by Bitok.

Development portfolio

Project ideas and interventions

PROJECT 01

City mobility

Evaluate junction safety, non-motorised transport, public transport circulation and congestion bottlenecks.

Potential partners: City board, transport agencies, road authorities
PROJECT 02

Markets and trading spaces

Assess vending facilities, sanitation, drainage and access for traders and buyers.

Potential partners: Traders, city management, public health
PROJECT 03

Urban drainage and waste

Prioritise storm-water hotspots, solid-waste handling and flood risk mapping.

Potential partners: Water and environment teams
PROJECT 04

Business and investment districts

Identify infrastructure constraints around commercial clusters and service access.

Potential partners: Business groups, planners, investors
PROJECT 05

Digital city services

Scope online permit guidance, complaint tracking and transparent progress reporting.

Potential partners: ICT teams, public agencies
Turning ideas into action

Suggested implementation roadmap

  1. 1Publish issues raised by residents and businesses
  2. 2Review existing Eldoret city plans before making new promises
  3. 3Undertake costed designs and environmental assessments
  4. 4Coordinate responsibilities across city, county and national entities
  5. 5Track safety, service quality and business-access indicators
