Terms & Conditions

These terms explain the core rules for using BeatHub, including creator earnings and scheduled withdrawals.

01

Marketplace purchases

By using BeatHub you agree to our marketplace rules. Exclusive purchases grant the applicable exclusive rights and are removed from sale after confirmed payment. Non-exclusive licenses may be sold to multiple buyers according to the license presented at checkout.

02

Creator earnings

BeatHub retains its disclosed platform commission on completed sales. The remaining eligible amount is credited to the creator's account as earnings. A displayed balance may include funds that are pending, reserved or otherwise unavailable for withdrawal.

03

Creator withdrawals

Creator withdrawals are not instant. BeatHub processes eligible, approved creator withdrawals on Tuesday and Thursday, using East Africa Time (EAT), with the scheduled payout window ending at 6:00 PM EAT.

The minimum creator withdrawal is KSh 500. Requests below this amount are not accepted. A request submitted after the applicable payout-day cutoff is carried forward to the next scheduled payout cycle.

04

Review and approval

Every creator withdrawal is subject to BeatHub's administrative review before payment. BeatHub may approve, hold or reject a request where the account, transaction history, available balance, M-Pesa destination, payment records or other information requires verification.

Approval means the request has passed BeatHub's review. The payout is considered completed only after BeatHub records the M-Pesa payment as paid and records the applicable payout reference.

05

M-Pesa details and payment processing

Creators are responsible for providing a correct, active Kenyan M-Pesa number. BeatHub is not responsible for delays or failed delivery caused by incorrect recipient information, M-Pesa service interruptions, banking or telecommunications issues, or other circumstances outside BeatHub's reasonable control.
