Community Guidelines

Share feelings, not identities

Do not publish full names, phone numbers, addresses or details intended to identify another person.

No harassment

No threats, targeted abuse, doxxing, hate, sexual exploitation or encouragement of harm. Report content that violates these rules.

Protect vulnerable people

Do not use Unsaid to track, expose or pressure someone into contact or reconciliation. Reconciliation is always optional.

Immediate danger

Unsaid is not emergency or professional mental-health care. If you are in immediate danger or may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a trusted person now.
