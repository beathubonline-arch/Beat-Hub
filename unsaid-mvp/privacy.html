Privacy at Unsaid

Anonymous means anonymous

Public stories are not searchable by phone number and should never contain identifying details. Unsaid does not reveal a public story author to another user.

Private letters

A recipient can receive a private letter only when the writer explicitly chooses to send it. Public anonymous stories remain separate.

Safety and data

We aim to collect only what is needed to operate the service, protect users, prevent abuse and understand aggregate product performance. Do not post addresses, phone numbers, full names or other identifying information in public stories.

Your control

Users should be able to report unsafe content and control whether their writing remains private, is shared anonymously, or is deliberately sent to someone.

This MVP privacy notice will be expanded before production accounts, payments or sensitive-data processing are enabled.
