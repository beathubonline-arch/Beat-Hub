# BeatHub 30-Day Acquisition System

## Objective
Turn the Growth Agent into a repeatable, human-reviewed acquisition loop for independent artists and creators.

## Operating loop
1. Scout public prospects using public information only.
2. Qualify prospects against BeatHub's target audience.
3. Match qualified prospects to real BeatHub catalogue items.
4. Generate a personalized outreach draft.
5. Human reviews and approves before any contact.
6. Record contact and advance the funnel only when the action actually happened.
7. Track visits, registrations, plays, connections, and purchases.
8. Publish weekly content that drives discovery back to BeatHub.
9. Review weekly funnel conversion and fix the largest bottleneck.

## 30-day cadence

### Week 1 — Foundation and discovery
- Day 1: Baseline analytics, catalogue health, and campaign setup.
- Day 2: Scout and qualify first prospect batch.
- Day 3: Match prospects to relevant beats.
- Day 4: Prepare personalized outreach drafts.
- Day 5: Human review and first outreach batch.
- Day 6: Publish first beat-focused short-form content batch.
- Day 7: Review visits, signups, plays, replies, and pipeline quality.

### Week 2 — Activation
- Day 8: Scout second prospect batch.
- Day 9: Match and prioritize highest-fit prospects.
- Day 10: Outreach review and approval.
- Day 11: Publish artist/problem-solving content.
- Day 12: Follow up only with prospects where a follow-up is appropriate.
- Day 13: Publish beat showcase content.
- Day 14: Weekly funnel review and bottleneck selection.

### Week 3 — Conversion
- Day 15: Identify strongest-performing beat/content/prospect segments.
- Day 16: Scout a focused batch around those segments.
- Day 17: Match and prepare outreach.
- Day 18: Human review and outreach.
- Day 19: Publish social proof / producer spotlight content.
- Day 20: Improve marketplace-to-checkout conversion based on observed behavior.
- Day 21: Weekly funnel review.

### Week 4 — Scale what works
- Day 22: Repeat highest-performing prospect source.
- Day 23: Repeat highest-performing content format.
- Day 24: Match prospects to best-converting catalogue segments.
- Day 25: Outreach review and approval.
- Day 26: Publish strongest beat/artist content format.
- Day 27: Retention and repeat-buyer review.
- Day 28: Full funnel review.
- Day 29: Document winning acquisition playbook.
- Day 30: Measure the month, select next month's experiments, and archive results.

## Weekly content baseline
- 1 long-form YouTube video per week.
- 5 short-form videos per week.
- Beat showcase content.
- Producer/creator spotlight content.
- Artist education content.
- Every piece has a clear BeatHub discovery CTA.

## Guardrails
- Public information only.
- No private-data scraping.
- No spam automation.
- No automatic outbound publishing or messaging.
- Human approval is required before outreach is recorded as contacted.
- Do not invent catalogue items, prices, prospects, or outcomes.

## Core metrics
Visitors, signups, active users, prospects found, qualified prospects, outreach-ready prospects, contacts, replies, visits, registrations, beat plays, connections, checkout starts, purchases, revenue, and repeat purchases.

## Decision rule
At each weekly review, identify the largest measurable conversion loss and run the smallest useful experiment against it. Do not add features merely to increase feature count.
